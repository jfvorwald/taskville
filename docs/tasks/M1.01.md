## Additions beyond ARCHITECTURE.md (not contradictions, flagged for the reviewer)

- Routes `POST /v1/projects` (FR-011) and `POST /v1/pairing`, `POST /v1/pairing/revoke` (section 13 implies them) are not in the section 10 table. #110 D2 asks whether to add them there.

## Lessons
