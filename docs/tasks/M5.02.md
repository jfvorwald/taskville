## Implementation notes

- `apps/web/src/city/crew/subagents/`: `generation.ts`, `crew.ts`, `lifecycle.ts` are the pinned pure modules (`lifecycle.ts` also exports `stillFrame` for reduced motion and an `outward` field on the frame). `tracker.ts` maps store agents to families (grouped by immediate parent; running agents below generation 2 are counted on their generation-2 ancestor) and records the first seen start and finish on the store clock. `spots.ts` is the arc layout. `resources.ts` pools the merged bot geometry, stepped-opacity materials and pill sprites. `SubagentCrew.tsx` is the R3F layer; `index.ts` re-exports.
- A subagent first seen already finished never walks. The result cube, hand-over and fade are timed from the finish seen in the store, not from the event's own timestamp.
- Reduced motion: bots jump between the spot and the door, no hop, and vanish without fading.
- Mount: one import and one element in `apps/web/src/city/index.tsx` (`<SubagentCrew reducedMotion={reducedMotion} />`, after `CityAgents`).
- Reuses `agentColors`, `sharedGeometries`, `colorMaterial`, `agentScale`, `emoteMaterial` and `agentAnchorId` from the existing city code. The parent identity text for the trim mirrors `identityOf` in `movement/models.ts` (not exported, so repeated in `tracker.ts`).
- The "..." over the parent shows while any subagent walks out or works, or any is too deep for a bot. The marker click only fans the bots out; the parent's crew list is not part of this ticket.
- Untested fix commits: fe4722d, 97945c4, 92d642f (later pinned by the test-writer in a900450), d30ca5a (marker hue, no test), f3154c2 (a subagent with no known parent keeps its block view, no test).
- Round 2: `buildModels` skips the view for a subagent whose parent is in the store and exports `identityOf` (used by `tracker.ts`). `tint.ts` gives a crew bot the red or amber hue a block agent's ring has; `SubagentCrew` paints the bot's body and trim with it from `useData(s => s.attention)`, and the "+N crew" marker takes the most urgent hue of its hidden bots. Draw calls added in round 2: none.

## Known limitations

- Bots stand on an arc in front of the parent, not at a building door (the city exposes no door position).
- The accessible agent list and the world labels are built from `CityAgents` views, so subagents with a known parent no longer appear in either. Labels: accepted by the CTO. The agent list: open; fix is for `CityAgents` to publish label rows for subagents (outside this ticket's paths).
- A subagent's attention bubble and celebration effects anchor at its navigator position (`agent:<id>`, published by `CityAgents`), not at the crew bot. Fix is to publish the crew bot's position as that anchor and stop `CityAgents` overwriting it (outside this ticket's paths).
- `CityAgents` caches the last block view of an agent in `lastViews`; an agent first seen as a placeholder and later registered as a subagent can keep a stale block bot until navigation retires it (edge case, outside this ticket's paths).
- The marker's tint shows urgency, but the hidden bot itself is only visible after fan-out.
