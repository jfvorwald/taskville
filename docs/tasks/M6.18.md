## Approach

`apps/engine/test/timelines/checkpoints-followups.test.ts`: the two assertions with a 400 ms bound ("foreign-lock path" and "the budget is shared") now use `BUDGET_CEILING_MS = 2500`, half the 5000 ms busy timeout, documented at the constant. The existing `lockCost` helper (minimum of 3 locked runs minus minimum of 3 unlocked runs) is kept. The tighter bounds (100 and 130 ms, for "no wait at all" cases) were not part of the flake and are unchanged.

`archives-review.test.ts` has no wall-clock assertion (its only `toBeLessThanOrEqual` counts events), so it is unchanged.

## Follow-up: roborev 7859 (low)

It counts statements, not time, so load cannot move it. Ceilings kept.
