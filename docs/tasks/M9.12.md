- **Root cause:** break activities last 2 to 5 minutes and the next one can send an agent walking mid-sample, after M9.11's settle. A walk puts the render pacer at full rate (by design), which SwiftShader renders at about 27 fps: CI's 26.3 and 27.2. The product requests no frames without visible change.
- **Fix:** the spec watches movement on every animation frame during the sample (walking, arriving, departing, on the way), discards a window anyone moved in and resamples after a fresh settle (120 s cap), asserts the movement hook exists, and moves the pointer before settling. The 25 fps limit is unchanged.
- **Lessons:** a settle before a sample proves stillness only at its start; check stillness throughout the window and resample a window that moved. Low, not fixed: about 1 frame per second comes from React commits re-applying props (likely `CityAgents` on the break plan's 1 s tick), within budget, filed as a follow-up. Investigation by the debugger.
