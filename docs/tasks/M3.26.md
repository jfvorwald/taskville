- **Lessons:**
  - The flakes were not background-tab throttling: the page stayed visible and requestAnimationFrame ran at 60 Hz until a single 4 to 4.5 s stall. The cause is software WebGL (SwiftShader) shared by every test page in one Chromium: with files running in parallel, R3F drew 0 frames in a window where it draws 38 alone. Any wait capped at `vi.waitFor`'s 1 s default, or a fixed frame count, loses to that stall.
  - Drive frames by hand (`frameloop="never"` and `advance()`) when a test is about animation over time, and wait on the condition (root exists, a frame was drawn) with a generous bound otherwise.
