The city passes the DataStore's clock: `DataStore` exposes a read-only `now` (`apps/web/src/data/store.ts`, the store's existing injected clock), `CityAgents` hands `store.now` to `AgentsLayer`, which hands it to `AgentBlock` and the hook.
